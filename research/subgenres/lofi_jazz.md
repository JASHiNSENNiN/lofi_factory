## Harmony
The most harmonically dense of the 13 — full jazz vocabulary rather than a stripped-down subset: ii-V-I / ii-V-i cadences extended to 9ths, 11ths, 13ths (e.g. iim9-V7#9b13-Imaj9), secondary dominants, and chromatic passing chords borrowed straight from bebop/cool-jazz harmony, just held static for bars instead of moving at jazz tempo. Compared to jazz_cafe (which leans bossa/smooth-jazz simplicity) and chillhop (which keeps voicings consonant), lofi_jazz should carry the most chromatic tension and the widest chord-extension palette.

## Melody
Melodic phrasing draws on real jazz solo/head vocabulary — scalar runs, chromatic approach tones, occasional blue-note bends — restrained to fit the lofi loop aesthetic (a "head" phrase repeated and lightly varied rather than improvised at length). Vibraphone/muted-trumpet leads favor legato lines that outline extended chord tones (9ths, 13ths) rather than simple triads, distinguishing it from chillhop's more diatonic melodic language.

## Rhythm/groove
Swing feel is central and audibly "jazz" — brushed snare, upright-style bass following chord roots with a laid-back attack, hi-hats/ride carrying a loose swing pulse. Vinyl crackle and tape hiss layered continuously at low volume are a genre-defining texture, distinguishing the "sampled from an old jazz record" aesthetic from the cleaner, more hi-fi chillhop palette.

## Arrangement/structure
Loop-based like the rest of the lofi family, but drawing more explicitly on jazz-combo instrumentation layering: rhythm section (bass + brushed drums) establishes the pocket, comping piano/Rhodes chords enter next, then a lead voice (vibraphone, muted trumpet, or sax) states the melodic hook — closely mirroring a small-combo head-arrangement rather than a beat-first hip-hop structure.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — general lofi-jazz production guides and jazz-harmony references (Hooktheory, Learn Jazz Standards) rather than a codified generative algorithm.
