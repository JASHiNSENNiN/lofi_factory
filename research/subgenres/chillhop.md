## Harmony
Chillhop leans directly on jazz's ii-V-I (in a minor key, ii-V-i), e.g. Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7, but holds each chord for a full bar or more instead of racing through changes the way a jazz standard does. Extended chords are the norm — maj7, m7, dominant 9ths — rather than plain triads. A common extension is ii-V-I-IV (Dm7-G7-Cmaj7-Fmaj7) to lengthen the loop. Chillhop's harmony is closer to neo-soul-adjacent chillhop than to the more chromatic bebop-derived jazz cafe/lofi jazz palette — voicings stay diatonic and consonant, favoring warmth over tension. Borrowed chords are used sparingly (occasional bVII or IV-iv "plagal" color) rather than constant modal interchange.

## Melody
Contour is smooth and stepwise with modest leaps, phrased in 2-4 bar calls that leave space (chillhop favors "sparse and repeating" melodic material to keep the track calm rather than busy). Lead lines sit on Rhodes/nylon guitar/flute/sax and imply the harmony directly — chord-tone landings on downbeats, passing tones on the "and" of beats. Compared to hip_hop_lofi's grittier trumpet-led lines, chillhop melody is cleaner and more legato.

## Rhythm/groove
Swing is present but restrained — enough offset that the beat doesn't feel mechanically quantized like house or techno, but not as heavy as boom-bap. Syncopated yet unhurried drum patterns with warm, rounded bass. Ghost hi-hat notes and light drum-break layering (kick/snare/hats/percussion tracked separately) are typical of the production approach, giving a live-feeling pocket without J Dilla-style extreme micro-timing drift.

## Arrangement/structure
Typical form is a loop-based instrumental structure: short intro (often just drums or a filtered chord), then a steady main loop with periodic drop-outs (removing drums or bass for 4-8 bars) for variation, building back up before a short outro. Layering order is usually drums/bass foundation first, then chords (Rhodes/guitar), then lead melody and light pad/texture on top. Tracks run in the 1.5-3 minute "vignette" range typical of the wider lofi ecosystem.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — no distinct generative-music repos target chillhop specifically; it inherits general lofi-hip-hop production tutorials (Splice, EDMProd, LANDR-style guides).
