## Harmony
Study lofi's defining harmonic trait, per multiple production guides, is deliberate simplicity: "2-4 chords in total with little to no variation," commonly built from "just the white keys, in C major or A minor," with 7th chords as the primary color addition (9ths/11ths only once 7ths are comfortable). This should be the least harmonically adventurous profile in the roster short of bedroom_pop — no dense jazz substitutions, no dramatic modal shifts, just a steady maj7/m7 loop that repeats without variation long enough to become background rather than foreground. The functional goal (noise-masking, non-distracting) directly argues against the "surprising" chord voicings appropriate to lofi_jazz or city_pop.

## Melody
Melodic fragments should repeat "without dramatic build-ups," staying sparse and predictable — marimba/mallet or Rhodes-based short motifs that loop steadily rather than develop through the codebase's climax-arc mechanism as aggressively as other subgenres. The functional research (masking distraction, supporting sustained attention) implies melody should recede rather than demand attention: lower density, fewer wide leaps, minimal tension-arc dynamics compared to more emotionally expressive subgenres like dark_lofi or neo_soul.

## Rhythm/groove
Consistency is the explicit, research-backed requirement: "skip tracks with sudden volume spikes or abrupt beat changes... the goal is to blend into the background." Sparse, unhurried percussion with steady, predictable pocket — no dramatic fills, no aggressive syncopation, no energy swings. This directly informs keeping the swing band tight and the energy setting at 'medium' (as the current code already does) rather than 'high' — study_lofi should feel like the most rhythmically "safe" and repeatable profile of the whole 26, engineered for tune-out-ability rather than musical interest.

## Arrangement/structure
Loop-based with minimal arrangement drama — steady tempo throughout, no abrupt transitions, avoiding the "surprises" (drops, tempo shifts, vocal hooks) that source material explicitly identifies as counterproductive to focus. Structurally this argues for a flatter dynamic arc across a track's duration than more emotionally-driven subgenres; the engine's per-track tension arc should stay compressed/narrow-range for this key rather than building toward a strong climax.

## Reference repos/algorithms
No dedicated study-lofi algorithmic generator repos found, but the functional research base (PLOS ONE 2017 attention study, Journal of Adolescence 2024 anxiety study) gives concrete, actionable BPM/consistency targets unusual among the more purely aesthetic subgenre profiles — worth treating as the roster's one "evidence-based" tuning target rather than a purely stylistic one. Channels like Chillhop Music / Lofi Girl are the closest "reference catalog," valued specifically for maintaining steady mid-tempo grooves without disruptive variation.
