## Harmony
Chill beats favors the most minimal harmonic movement in the whole lofi family — many tracks loop just two chords for the entire runtime (a vi-i or i-VI two-chord cycle is a common template), leaning on extended 7th/9th voicings held static for bars at a time rather than moving through changes. Where three or four chords are used, parallel/planing motion (chords a step or semitone apart) is common — the harmonic interest comes from voicing color and reverb tail, not chord variety. This is the most stripped-down harmonic language of the 13 subgenres — deliberately more minimal than chillhop or lofi_jazz.

## Melody
Melody is sparse by design — "kept sparse and repeating to maintain focus and calm." Long tones, wide space between phrases, minimal rhythmic activity; when a lead appears (vibraphone in the current config) it tends toward slow arpeggiated figures or single sustained motifs rather than active phrase development. Density should be the lowest of the "hip-hop lofi" cluster.

## Rhythm/groove
Groove is built more through sidechain compression (kick ducking the pads/bass to create pulse) than through swung note placement — sidechaining is explicitly emphasized as a genre-typical technique for making tracks "groove organically" while keeping the mix spacious. Drums are minimal/soft with light hats; a "vignette" arrangement approach with occasional dropout sections is standard, and the swing that is present is subtle (a 15-20% feel) rather than the heavy Dilla-style swing of hip_hop_lofi.

## Arrangement/structure
Short-form (1.5-3 min) vignette structure: brief intro/outro, minimal variation across the body, occasional dropout sections (drums or bass cut for several bars) rather than distinct verse/chorus. Layering is foundation-first (pad/bass), then sparse chords, then occasional lead — with ambient textures (rain, room tone, vinyl) filling headroom left by the minimal arrangement.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — general downtempo/ambient-lofi production guides (LANDR, Splice) rather than a codified generative approach.
