## Harmony
Bedroom pop favors simple, often minimal chord vocabulary — sources note whole songs built on just a two-chord VI-i loop, relying on "texture, rhythm, and arrangement rather than harmonic movement." Where more motion exists, I-IV-V variants and modal-interchange/suspended chords appear, but the guiding principle is simplicity over jazz sophistication — a clear step down in harmonic density from cozy_cafe or jazz_cafe. Occasional passing chords (e.g., a quick half-diminished ii between two majors) add mild "outside" color reflecting the genre's "bending the rules... weird chords you'd never hear on Top 40" indie ethos, but these should be used sparingly, not as a constant jazz-turnaround habit.

## Melody
Melody here is a stand-in for the genre's real focus — introspective, often melancholic vocal lines — which in this instrumental context should translate to a gentle guitar-nylon or vibraphone melody with soft, half-formed phrasing: short motifs that trail off rather than complete arcs, echoing "diary entry" intimacy over polished songcraft. Major pentatonic keeps it warm without slipping into full jazz color; avoid the confident, extroverted runs appropriate to summer_vibes or city_pop.

## Rhythm/groove
Drums should be deliberately unassertive — sources are explicit that "the golden rule for bedroom pop drums is don't be assertive," favoring soft, muffled snares and punchy-but-not-overpowering kicks that stay in a supporting role. Timing looseness (roughly 60-80% quantize equivalent) rather than a pronounced swing groove is the authentic feel — closer to "human imperfection" than to a deliberate shuffle, distinguishing this from the more overtly swung jazz-cafe cluster.

## Arrangement/structure
DIY, home-recording aesthetic: modest "room sound," minimal polish, tape hiss/vinyl crackle layered in for warmth, and a general embrace of imperfection as an asset rather than a flaw. Structurally, arrangements should stay sparse and low-density — fewer simultaneous layers than cozy_cafe or city_pop — letting the (implied) intimate vocal/melody carry the track over a lightly-sketched instrumental bed.

## Reference repos/algorithms
No dedicated bedroom-pop MIDI/algorithmic generator repos found; the genre's tradition is fundamentally artist/DIY-production-driven (Clairo, beabadoobee, Rex Orange County, boy pablo, Mac DeMarco) rather than algorithmic. Closest technique-level reference is the broader "lo-fi guitar chord voicing" and "bedroom production" tutorial ecosystem — useful for calibrating the deliberately imperfect, low-density arrangement approach rather than any specific generative pattern.
