## Harmony
Rooted in minor-key jazz/soul harmony: the ii-V-i cadence (e.g. Dm7b5 - G7 - Cm7 in a minor context) is a foundation, often simplified to a 2-3 chord loop (i-iv, i-VI, or i-bVII-iv) since "simplicity is strength" in boom-bap-derived hip-hop. Borrowed dominant-7 and m7b5 (half-diminished) chords add jazz color without moving through full changes. Chords are commonly sampled/chopped from soul records rather than played through — voicings favor closed, mid-register clusters that sit well under a sample-chop aesthetic.

## Melody
Melodic material is often secondary to the loop/sample itself — short 2-4 note motifs restated with variation, muted trumpet or Rhodes leads that sit above the drum pocket rather than dominating it. Phrasing follows the "head-nod" logic: melody enters late in the bar, leaves space on beat 1, and repeats with minor variation rather than developing extensively.

## Rhythm/groove
This is the deepest swing pocket in the hip-hop-lofi family, directly descended from J Dilla's "Dilla Time" — per-element (not global) micro-timing offsets, e.g. kick early by ~30ms, snare dragging by ~20ms, applied independently per drum voice rather than uniformly. The MPC's per-sound swing control (rather than one swing % for the whole kit) is the defining production technique; boom-bap kick/snare pattern with off-grid hats. Ghost snare hits and dusty, muffled drum-machine texture (bitcrushed, downsampled) are genre-defining.

## Arrangement/structure
Sample-based loop structure: a 2-4 bar core loop (drums + chopped chord/melody sample) repeats with periodic breakdowns (drums-only, or melody-only) roughly every 8-16 bars, plus a filtered/muted intro and outro. Vinyl crackle and tape hiss run continuously under the whole track at low level rather than being a discrete "section." Layering order: drum break/kick-snare foundation first, then bass, then the chopped melodic sample/chord loop, then sparse fills/ad-lib textures on top.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — inherits general MPC/boom-bap sampling and swing-programming tutorials rather than any codified generative algorithm.
