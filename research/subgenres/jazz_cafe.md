## Harmony
Leans toward smooth-jazz and bossa-adjacent harmony rather than lofi_jazz's denser bebop vocabulary: major 7th chords as tonic (rather than minor), smooth voice leading (minimal movement between chord tones), and bossa-derived progressions like Imaj7-II7-iim7-V7-Imaj7 or the classic ii-V-I. Major 7ths carry "warm, comforting" color central to jazz_cafe's brighter, more optimistic harmonic identity — a clear contrast to dark_lofi/lofi_phonk's minor/phrygian world and even brighter than lofi_jazz's more chromatic minor-leaning palette.

## Melody
Gentle, singable lines — soft tenor-sax-style melody or light piano comping figures rather than the chromatic bebop runs of lofi_jazz. Phrases are unhurried and consonant, built to sit in the background of a "cozy coffee shop" listening context rather than demand foreground attention; melodic leaps are rare, stepwise motion and long tones dominate.

## Rhythm/groove
Light brush snare and a gentle piano-comping pulse define the pocket — less aggressive swing than lofi_jazz, more "easy listening jazz" than "bebop-derived lofi." Some jazz_cafe production explicitly experiments with 3/4 time for a more "romantic," less hip-hop-groove feel, though 4/4 with soft swing remains the default. Arrangement can evolve through subtle filter sweeps and "ghost kicks" so it feels alive yet calmly repetitive rather than static.

## Arrangement/structure
Designed for continuous, unobtrusive background listening (coffee-shop ambience) — long-form, gently evolving loops with minimal dramatic structure; layering favors piano/Rhodes comping and gentle bass first, with saxophone/trumpet/guitar/vibraphone leads woven in and out rather than a single fixed lead throughout.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — inherits general smooth-jazz/lofi production guides rather than a codified generative model.
