## Harmony
Deliberately minimal and loop-driven — phonk "rarely uses the kind of moving chord progressions you'd analyze in a jazz or pop tune"; harmony is a short, dark, repeating minor-key loop rather than a developing progression. Phrygian mode (the altered natural minor with the flat-2nd) gives the genre its "eerily exotic" quality and should be a primary, not secondary, scale choice — more central to lofi_phonk's identity than to dark_lofi, since phonk's melodic content is built almost entirely around a single cowbell riff mapped to a scale via the circle of fifths. Minimal chord changes reinforce the genre's hypnotic, repetitive character.

## Melody
The pitched, distorted 808 cowbell functions AS the melody instrument in real phonk — a melodic device essentially unique to this subgenre among the 13, and one the current code has no equivalent GM instrument for (closest available would be a woodblock/cowbell-adjacent percussive timbre, not the GM_ORGAN_ROCK currently assigned as 'melody'). The cowbell riff is mapped chromatically like a synth lead and bit-crushed/distorted for a sharp, metallic, "stabby" texture — this is a fundamentally different melodic idiom from every other subgenre here, which use sustained-tone instruments (Rhodes, vibraphone, trumpet, flute).

## Rhythm/groove
Built on Memphis rap's foundation: ominous minor-key loops, deep 808 sub-bass, and relentlessly simple, heavy drum programming, historically raw and cassette-mixtape-textured (hiss/distortion treated as features, not flaws). Triplet flows (a rhythmic cell borrowed from Memphis rap vocal delivery, applied to hi-hats/melodic phrasing) are a genre-defining rhythmic signature not really present elsewhere in this set. The genre splits into two moods worth distinguishing for a "lofi_phonk" (slower, more atmospheric) profile: the "Dirt" style (horror-synths, aggressive distortion, closer to dark_lofi) vs. the "Rare" style (smoother, jazzier samples pioneered by DJ Smokey/Soudiere) — lofi_phonk as a slowed hybrid likely sits closer to "Rare" phonk's jazzier, less aggressive end.

## Arrangement/structure
Loop-and-hypnotize structure — a short cowbell/bass/drum loop repeats with minimal variation, prioritizing hypnotic repetition over development (explicitly "gives phonk its hallmark repetitive and hypnotic feeling"). Layering: 808 sub-bass and simple kick/snare foundation first, then the pitched cowbell melodic riff, then minimal atmospheric/sample texture (cassette hiss, vocal chop) on top — a much sparser arrangement philosophy than the jazz-descended subgenres in this set.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — general phonk-production tutorials (cowbell-as-synth mapping, Memphis-rap sampling technique) rather than a codified generative model.
