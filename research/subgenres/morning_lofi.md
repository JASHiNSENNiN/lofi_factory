## Harmony
Morning lofi leans into major-key brightness more deliberately than most lofi subgenres — sources note major keys give "a refreshing, uplifting touch" as contrast to lofi's default melancholy. Extended maj7/maj9 chords (e.g., Cmaj9 into Fmaj13) are cited as giving progressions "a bright, uplifting start," and adding a 9th onto a maj7 chord specifically reads as "very uplifting... perfect for happier jams." Compared to cozy_cafe's warm-but-slightly-wistful maj7/m7 mix, morning_lofi should minimize minor-7th usage and favor pure major-7th/major-9th color with simple, songful I-IV-V-adjacent motion — gentle "waking up" harmony rather than jazz-club sophistication.

## Melody
Acoustic-leaning melodic voices (guitar-style plucks, flute, light piano) fit best — sources repeatedly pair morning lofi with acoustic guitar and flute over Rhodes/EP jazz-club timbre. Melodic phrasing should be simple and songlike (not motif-heavy jazz improvisation), major-pentatonic-forward, with a rising contour tendency evoking "sunrise" — gentle ascending phrases rather than the introspective downward-drifting lines more typical of dark_lofi or lofi_rnb.

## Rhythm/groove
Light, unhurried groove — soft kick/snare with gentle swing, lower density and lower velocity than cozy_cafe or study_lofi, evoking a slow wake-up rather than focused momentum. No aggressive syncopation or dense hi-hat rolls; morning_lofi should feel like the quietest, most spacious member of the "bright/cozy" cluster at the start of the day, contrasting with anime_lofi and summer_vibes which can afford more rhythmic energy.

## Arrangement/structure
Gradual, gentle builds rather than dramatic arrangement contrast — mirrors the "pervasive sense of optimism" and "coffee aroma and morning sunlight" imagery in source descriptions. Instrumentation commonly cited alongside morning lofi includes acoustic/electric guitar, bass, synths, pads, piano/EP, organ, light drums and percussion, and strings — a fuller, gentler palette than the sparser bedroom_pop or piano_lofi. Keep dynamic range narrow and avoid hard-hitting fills; the mood should stay consistently soft throughout.
