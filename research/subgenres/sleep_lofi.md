## Harmony
Sources are consistent that "deep sleep music... uses soft, repetitive melodies and leans heavily on ambient soundscapes rather than strong chord progressions" — harmony should be the most minimal and static of any candidate subgenre: a single sustained drone or 2-chord loop held far longer than even this codebase's `ambient` key uses, functioning as "a continuous, unwavering tonal foundation beneath the melody, staying the same pitch from beginning to end." Avoid any harmonic surprise, secondary dominant, or modal-interchange color — predictability is the explicit functional goal, more absolute here than in `study_lofi`.

## Melody
Melody should be reduced to the barest minimum — "soft melodies and very light instrumentation," with long-held, softly repeating phrases rather than any motif-development arc. Nature-inspired variants cite gentle fingerpicked guitar or soft Rhodes at very sparse density; the instrument palette should favor smooth-envelope, non-percussive-attack voices (pad, soft EP, muted mallet) since "music built from smooth-envelope instruments is fundamentally less arousing than music with percussive attack-heavy sounds" — a concrete, actionable production principle distinguishing this from every other subgenre in the roster.

## Rhythm/groove
Percussion should be minimal-to-absent — sources explicitly warn against "sudden percussion" and recommend "no unexpected breaks or beats" since the entire point is to avoid jolting a drifting-toward-sleep listener. Where a pulse exists at all, it should be the softest, most muffled, and least insistent of the whole roster (muffled kick/snare cited for nature-lofi-sleep variants) — swing should read as barely perceptible rather than a deliberate groove device, reinforcing the tightest, straightest swing band of any key.

## Arrangement/structure
Extremely minimalistic structure — "minimalistic structure with layered electric piano and synthesizer pads that blend into one another," designed to be "pleasant yet easy to tune out." No dynamic arc, no climax, no section contrast — a genuinely flat, unchanging texture for the track's duration is the correct target, more extreme than `ambient`'s already-minimal arrangement philosophy. Sources also note tempos below ~60 BPM can begin to feel "eerie or unsettling" rather than more relaxing — informing the 58 floor here (not lower) despite "sleep" branding suggesting an even slower number.

## Reference repos/algorithms
No dedicated algorithmic sleep-lofi generator repos found; the nearest technical parallel is binaural-beat/isochronic-tone generator scripts (frequency-difference-between-ears tools, widely available as small open-source utilities) which operate on a completely different signal-generation principle than this codebase's MIDI-based approach and would need to be layered separately rather than encoded via `_SUBGENRE_CONFIG`. Curated catalogs (Spotify "lofi sleep," Chill Moon Music, Chilled Cat Music) are the closest available "reference corpus" for calibrating the extreme-minimalism target.
