## Harmony
Nujabes' signature is modal jazz harmony rather than functional ii-V-I motion — static vamps around one or two chords per the modal-jazz tradition (Dorian, Mixolydian, Lydian modes), with harmonic interest concentrated in the melody/sample choice rather than chord movement. His chord voicings lean heavily on minor 7ths, 9ths, and 11ths for a melancholic-yet-warm atmosphere. Because his tracks are built from sampled jazz/soul/world-music records (Pharoah Sanders, Miles Davis, Yusef Lateef), the "progressions" are often whatever loop the source recording offers rather than freely composed changes — this favors picking one rich chord (maj9, m11) and letting it breathe.

## Melody
Piano-driven melodic loops paired with hip-hop-atypical lead instruments — flute (quena), oboe, strings — rather than the Rhodes/vibraphone-centric palette of most other subgenres here. Melody is gentle and legato, often carried by a sampled instrumental hook rather than a newly composed lead line; phrases are melodic and emotionally direct rather than technically virtuosic, "provoking an emotional reaction beyond appreciative head-nodding."

## Rhythm/groove
Soft kicks, brushed snares, and subtle hi-hats create a relaxed, unhurried groove — noticeably gentler in attack than boom-bap-proper hip_hop_lofi despite similar tempo/swing numbers. Drum breaks are sampled, then edited/pitched into individual hits and reassembled, with consistent hi-hat/cymbal timbre reused across tracks as a sonic "glue." This is minimal-arrangement drumming in service of the melodic/harmonic sample, not a rhythm-forward groove.

## Arrangement/structure
Cinematic, soundtrack-informed structure (heavily shaped by his work scoring Samurai Champloo) — tracks often build and recede in intensity like a film cue rather than looping a fixed hip-hop beat throughout; layering favors bringing melodic elements (strings, flute, piano) in and out over a stable, understated drum/bass bed. This pairs well with the code's 'extended' song form (I-A-BR-B-O with generous loop counts) already assigned to nujabes.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — no codified generative algorithm exists for Nujabes' style; closest analog is general modal-jazz-sampling production guides.
