## Harmony
Directly descended from neo-soul's extended-chord vocabulary: minor 9th (m9) chords are described as "the heart of the genre" — smooth, introspective, soulful — alongside warm/open maj7 and jazzy-tension dominant 7(b9/b13) chords. The iii-vi-ii-V progression common to neo-soul/R&B fusion applies here too, and the V7b9 functions as the key emotional pivot chord resolving into minor tonics. Chromatic voice-leading (smooth common-tone movement between chords) is prioritized over dramatic harmonic movement — the "silky, flowing quality that defines the genre" comes from how little the individual voices move, not from harmonic surprise.

## Melody
Vocal-chop-centric melodic thinking even in instrumental form — the genre's modern production language treats melody as chopped/pitched vocal syllables ("ah," "oh," "ooh") tuned to the chord progression rather than a freely composed instrumental line. Where an instrumental lead carries the melody (muted trumpet in the current config), it should mimic that vocal-phrase logic: short, breathy, intimate phrases with space between them rather than continuous melodic development.

## Rhythm/groove
Modern lofi-rnb draws from the "Trapsoul" lineage (Bryson Tiller, PARTYNEXTDOOR) — R&B vocal sensibility over trap-adjacent, hi-hat-roll-inflected beats — a harder-edged rhythmic bed than pure neo-soul's live-band feel. Minimal, soft drum patterns with light, swung hi-hats create the "relaxed groove"; the swing setting itself is explicitly cited as a lever (e.g. ~20% swing at 70 BPM) for the genre's laid-back human feel.

## Arrangement/structure
Slow-jam structural logic: sparse verse-like sections built around a repeating chord loop, with a smooth, mellow, minimally-obtrusive bassline anchoring the mix rather than overpowering it — bass supports the melody/vocal-chop hook rather than driving the groove itself (unlike lo_fi_funk, where bass IS the groove). Vinyl-treated percussion and evocative atmospheric fragments (reversed textures, tape warble) round out the intimate, nocturnal mood.

## Reference repos/algorithms
None specific — general R&B/vocal-chop production tutorials rather than a codified generative model.
